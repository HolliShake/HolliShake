05 / context

A bit about me

I'm Philipp, a developer from the Philippines. I currently work at the Digital Transformation Office on a job-order basis, and I graduated from the University of Science and Technology of Southern Philippines.

My day-to-day is fullstack work: schemas, APIs, dashboards, mobile builds. My nights are for the stuff nobody asked for. I'd rather write my own compiler than read someone else's docs, and that habit is why there are eight languages on this page.

I code best alone, with the door closed and something compiling. I also collect cheat sheets and CS books, and I film some of the process on my YouTube channel, @GhostCommit.
